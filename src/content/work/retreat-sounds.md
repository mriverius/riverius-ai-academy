## The client

Basim and Ramsha Mirza created Retreat Sounds to bring meditations, classes and conversations on mindfulness, science, spirituality and healing into one calm place. The app puts that library in their listeners' pockets.

## What we built

An iPhone app, designed around listening:

- **Guided meditations** for stress, sleep, focus and letting go.
- **Courses and masterclasses** to build a lasting practice.
- **ZenCasts**, soothing talks and reflections.
- A **meditation timer** and **custom playlists**.
- **Offline downloads**, so a session never depends on the signal.
- **Progress tracking** with sessions, streaks and badges.
- **Subscriptions**: a free 7-day trial, then monthly, yearly or lifetime access.

## The result

Retreat Sounds is live on the App Store, free to download, with the full library unlocked by subscription.
