## The challenge

Every week, the admin team copied hundreds of WhatsApp messages with weights, grades and delivery dates by hand. The monthly board report took three days, and errors were common.

## What we built

- An assistant that reads delivery messages and organizes them into a shared sheet.
- Automatic checks that flag any value that doesn't match.
- A monthly report that builds itself and lands in the board's inbox.

## How the team adopted it

We trained five people in two hands-on sessions. Today they adjust the rules themselves when the season changes.

## The result

The team got back **12 hours a week**, and the monthly report went from **three days to four hours**.
