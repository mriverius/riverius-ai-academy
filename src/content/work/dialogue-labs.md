## The client

Dialogue Labs is a Miami company that builds and monetizes websites at scale, with a network of more than 2,000 domains across over 50 verticals. Its goal is to turn digital real estate into high-performing, fully automated web properties.

## The challenge

A domain business runs on four decisions, over and over: which domains to buy, how to manage the ones you own, who to sell them to, and how to make the rest earn. At the scale of thousands of domains, every one of those decisions needs to be made by the system, not by hand.

## What we built

**Digital Estates**, an AI ecosystem of connected apps that covers the whole life of a domain:

- **Acquire:** ingests domains and classifies the best ones to buy.
- **Manage:** runs the entire domain portfolio in one place.
- **Sell:** finds the businesses most interested in each domain and sells to them.
- **Monetize:** earns from domains through their traffic.

It's a web app built with **Next.js**, powered by a setup of **AI agents** built with ClawCode and other tools. The whole ecosystem is fully AI-automated.

## The result

Dialogue Labs runs domain acquisition, portfolio management, sales and monetization from a single, fully AI-automated ecosystem.
