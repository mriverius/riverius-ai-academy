## The client

Freelance Navigator is a coaching program for creative professionals who want to start and build a freelance business. Its main offer is a $3,000 program, and it sells through conversations: Meta Ads bring prospects into the DMs, and a call is the next step.

## The challenge

Every ad click starts a conversation, and conversations go cold fast. Each prospect needed a quick, personal reply, the right questions to see if the program was a fit, and a follow-up if they went quiet. By hand, that is hard to keep up with, and leads slip away. And for a coaching brand, a reply that sounds like a bot is worse than no reply.

## What we built

An AI appointment setter that runs the whole conversation, from the first message to a booked call:

- **Replies to every conversation started from Meta Ads**, the moment it starts.
- **Qualifies each prospect** for the $3,000 program through natural conversation, so only good fits reach the calendar.
- **Books a call** with every qualified prospect.
- **Follows up with people who stop responding**, to keep conversations active and move them forward.
- **Writes in the tone of voice of Carlos, the founder**, so conversations feel personal, not robotic.

## The result

The appointment setter **booked 6 calls in its first 48 hours** and fully automated Freelance Navigator's lead funnel. The team saw a noticeable boost in lead quality and overall efficiency.
