## The client

Yasser Aldubaikhi wanted a chatbot that could act as a personal lawyer for Saudi Arabia: ask a legal question in plain words, get a clear answer based on the country's laws and regulations.

## The challenge

A chatbot can only answer like a lawyer if it knows the law. That meant collecting Saudi Arabia's laws and regulations first, then making sure every answer comes from their actual text, not from what a language model happens to remember.

## What we built

LawAI, built in **Python** in three parts:

- **Web scrapers** that collect Saudi Arabia's laws and regulations.
- **A legal knowledge base** built with **LlamaIndex**, which indexes every text so the chatbot can find the articles that apply to each question.
- **The chatbot**, which uses **ChatGPT** to write a clear answer from the laws it retrieves, like a personal lawyer would.

## The result

In the client's words, the final product exceeded his expectations: an intelligent, responsive chatbot, seamlessly integrated with the scraping. The work significantly enhanced his business operations, and the partnership continues after launch.
