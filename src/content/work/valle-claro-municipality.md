## The challenge

Requests arrived by email, web form and in person. One person read every one and forwarded it to the right department. Responses took more than a week, and nobody knew the status of each case.

## What we built

- A classifier that identifies the request type and the department responsible.
- Plain-language draft replies that an officer reviews before sending.
- A simple board showing the status of every request.

## How the team adopted it

We worked with each department to define its categories. Human review stays on every reply, by design.

## The result

Response time dropped from **nine days to two**, and **70%** of requests are routed with no manual work.
