## The challenge

Teachers spent their weekends writing lesson plans and individual feedback for every student. Some were already using AI tools on their own, with no shared standards and no care for student data.

## What we built

- A planning assistant grounded in the school's curriculum and rubrics.
- Feedback templates each teacher adapts to their own style.
- Clear privacy rules: no personal student data leaves the school.

## How the team adopted it

We trained a pilot group of eight teachers. They coached the rest of the faculty over the following term.

## The result

Each teacher saves **5 hours a week** on average. Today, **38 teachers** use it every day.
