## The client

GROW AI builds and manages AI chat systems for eCommerce brands, turning social, website and ad traffic into sales. We joined their team to build the funnels for their brand clients.

## The challenge

Brands spend heavily on ads, content and email, but only a small share of that attention turns into a purchase. Shoppers comment, click and browse, then leave. Each brand needed funnels that answer every shopper instantly, on the channel they already use, and keep following up until they buy.

## What we built

Working alongside the GROW AI team, we built AI funnels across **Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, WhatsApp, email and SMS**. They:

- **Capture emails** from people who engage with a brand's posts, ads and messages.
- **Recommend products** inside the conversation.
- **Recover abandoned carts** and bring shoppers back to checkout.
- **Follow up automatically** with shoppers who haven't bought yet.
- **Offer discount codes** to move shoppers to the purchase.
- **Sell on value**, explaining what each product does for the shopper, not only its price.

## The result

For GROW AI's eCommerce clients, the funnels generated **over $70,000 in revenue in their first month**, and **over $100,000 in two months**.
